About

Haven works like a Discord server, but you host it yourself. Download the zip, run it, and it gives you a link your friends can use from a browser, the desktop app or the Android app.

There's no company in the middle. No account with us, no email or phone number, and nothing to pay for. Your messages, files and voice calls stay on your own server.

Haven has been in development since February 2026. It's a spare-time project and it'll always be free.

Many helpful people with a shared passion for free, unfiltered and private chat have come together to improve it. Every change is public, with a changelog updated every release.

Features

The short version. The guide covers everything.

Chat

  • Text channels, threads and forums
  • Voice chat
  • Screen sharing, with more than one stream at a time
  • Watch videos and listen to music together
  • Encrypted direct messages
  • Reactions, stickers, polls and GIFs

Coming from Discord

  • Import your whole server, including threads, pins and attachments
  • Ferry bridges a Haven channel to a Discord channel, so friends who stay behind can still talk to you
  • Roles and permissions that work the way you're used to

Customization

  • 26 themes with customizable effects, or write your own CSS
  • Bots and webhooks
  • Sign in with your own identity provider
  • Guest access, invite links and channel codes
  • Available in 8 languages

Extras

  • Rich Presence: show what you're playing or listening to
  • Built-in games with leaderboards
  • Flash games, through Ruffle
  • Backups and updates from the settings page

Setup

No terminal, no config files. There's a Docker image too.

  1. Download the zip and unzip it anywhere.
  2. Double-click Start Haven.bat (start.sh on Mac and Linux). It sets everything up and prints your link. If Node.js isn't installed yet, it offers to install it for you.
  3. Send the link to your friends. They can use a browser, the desktop app or the Android app.

To reach friends outside your home network you'll need to open a port or use a tunnel. The guide covers both, and there's a one-click tunnel in the admin settings.

Security and privacy

How it actually works.

  • Your data lives on the computer running the server, in one folder. Back it up, move it or delete it whenever you want.
  • Direct messages are end-to-end encrypted. The server stores them but can't read them, and neither can the admin.
  • Channel messages aren't end-to-end encrypted. Whoever runs the server can read them, just like a Discord admin can. Join servers run by people you trust, or run your own.
  • Connections use HTTPS. Haven creates its own certificate the first time it starts, and you can swap in a real one.
  • No telemetry. The server doesn't send usage data or crash reports anywhere.
  • There's no central Haven server and no Haven account. Nothing to leak.
