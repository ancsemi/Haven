Network access

How should friends connect to your server?

Port-Forwarding Steps:
1. Open your router admin (usually http://192.168.1.1)
2. Find "Port Forwarding" or "NAT" settings
3. Add rule: External 3000 → Internal 3000, TCP
4. Set Internal IP to this PC's local IP
5. Share your public IP: https://YOUR_IP:3000

Restore from a backup

Pick a backup made in Haven under Settings, Admin, Backup. It must have been made with Messages ticked.

  • Your accounts, channels, messages and settings come back, so you sign in with your existing account. There is no new admin to create.
  • Uploaded files come back too if the backup has them.
  • If this computer already has Haven data, it is kept beside the restored copy, not deleted.
